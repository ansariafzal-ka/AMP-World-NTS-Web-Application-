Summary of Key Features & Updates
  • Student's Instructions & Guidelines: Standalone guidelines component with complete schedule, reporting times, prohibited items, and FAQs mounted on Home Page with QuickNav tab badge.
  • Scholarships List Integration: Integrated direct link (www.tinyurl.com/ampntsscholarshipslist) to both Home Page and NTS Details scholarship cards.
  • Be a Part of AMP NTS: Redesigned compact role coordination banner with dedicated contacts for Ms. Nasim Ma'am and Ms. Zainab Batool.
  • Helpline & Navigation Alignment: Repositioned top navbar Helpline redirection next to Institute Registration.
  • Contact Page: Reordered sections to prioritize the Daily Helpline Meeting directly above Helpline Numbers.
  • Mobilization & Registration: Added MobilizationPartnerSection and streamlined exam center registration flows.
